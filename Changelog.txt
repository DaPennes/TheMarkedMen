== The Marked Men — Changelog ==

--- Added ---
- New settings: Infect colonists, Infect allies, Infect enemies. Choose which pawn groups can catch the Marked Virus.
- New setting: Ranged infection transmission. Infected pawns can now spread the virus through gunfire and ranged attacks (disabled by default).
- New setting: Ranged infection chance. Controls how likely a ranged attack is to transmit the virus.
- Full translation support for the entire settings window. Every label, tooltip, and section header is now ready for language packs.

--- Changed ---
- Presets renamed to clearer difficulty names: Very Easy, Easy, Normal, Hard, Very Hard.
- "Corpse reanimation" renamed to "corpse transformation" throughout the settings and UI text.
- Restrain and Release prisoner gizmo buttons now show English text instead of broken translation keys.
- Settings now check all faction types (colonists, prisoners, allies, enemies) separately before allowing infection.
- Active preset label now properly translates and handles old save data.
- Performance: Reduced how often infected pawns scan for targets, coordinate with each other, and check for nearby enemies.
- Performance: AI target selection now caches results briefly so the same pawn isn't rescanned every tick.

--- Fixed ---
- Fixed unescaped "&" characters in translation strings, which caused some settings section headers to display raw key text instead of readable labels.
