== The Marked Men — Changelog ==

--- Added ---
- New setting: Ranged infection transmission. Infected pawns can now spread the virus through gunfire and other ranged attacks (disabled by default).
- New setting: Ranged infection chance. Controls how likely a ranged attack is to transmit the virus.
- New settings: Infect colonists, Infect allies, Infect enemies. Choose exactly which groups of pawns can catch the Marked Virus.
- Full translation support for the entire settings window. Every label, tooltip, section header, and help text now works with language packs.
- Translation support for the Outbreak preset button label.

--- Changed ---
- Presets renamed to clearer difficulty names: Very Easy, Easy, Normal, Hard, Very Hard.
- "Corpse reanimation" renamed to "corpse transformation" everywhere in settings and UI text.
- Restrain and Release prisoner gizmo labels now show in English instead of missing translation keys.
- Settings window now checks all faction types (colonists, prisoners, allies, enemies) separately before allowing infection.
- Active preset label now works with translations and handles old save data.
- Performance: Reduced how often infected pawns scan for targets, coordinate with each other, and check for enemies.

--- Fixed ---
- Fixed broken translation strings where "&" was not properly escaped, causing some settings section headers to show raw key text instead of readable labels.
- Fixes code for description text.

--- Removed ---
- Hardcoded English strings in the settings window (replaced with translation keys throughout).
