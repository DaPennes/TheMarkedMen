=== The Marked Men - Changelog ===

New - Prisoner Restraint System:
----------------------------------
- You can now restrain infected prisoners on any bed
- Click the bed with an assigned infected prisoner and choose "Restrain"
- The prisoner walks to the bed and gets chained down
- Once on the bed, they can't move or do anything (like being under anesthesia)
- They stay there until you click "Release"
- Restrained prisoners won't attack wardens, hurt themselves, or try to escape
- The virus still progresses while restrained
- Can be turned on/off in mod settings under "Marked Prisoners"

Fixes:
------
- Restraint button now shows up on all beds properly
- Prisoner stays on the bed without trying to get up
- Restrained prisoner can actually reach the bed first (they don't get stuck)
- Custom button icon support added
