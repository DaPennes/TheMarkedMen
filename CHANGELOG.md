# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added
- Every kind of infected can now show up in raids, from civilians to Marked Men.
- Alphas, warlords, and Marked Men now feel tougher and hit harder.
- A countdown that shows when the next Marked Men raid will arrive.
- A warning letter about a day before a raid, with how dangerous it will be and when it should arrive.
- Marked Men can break through walls and chase colonists, preferring the wounded, the alone, and anyone running away.
- Large raids now smash a way in first, then spread out to hunt.

### Removed
- Catching the virus from infected corpses or bad food.
- Extra enemy-mix options that repeated the same settings.
- An old countdown display that was no longer used.
- Old behind-the-scenes work that was no longer needed.

### Changed
- Raids grow larger and more varied as your colony gets richer and harder to attack.
- Early raids are mostly ordinary infected; later raids bring more specialists and leaders.
- The difficulty settings now keep all infected types in play instead of turning most of them off.
- Only a few leaders can join each raid, and the strongest ones become more common in bigger raids.
- Raid letters now list every kind of infected in the attack.
- The countdown turns red when the raid is nearly here.
- You only get one warning letter per raid.
- Scheduled raids and hordes keep their own timing instead of being delayed.
- Large colonies now run more smoothly.

### Fixed
- Older saves no longer keep most raid types turned off after updating.
- The alpha limit now actually limits how many alphas can join.
- Several difficulty settings no longer turn off most infected types by mistake.
- Raids no longer arrive empty when the countdown ends.
- A raid that fails to start now tries again the next day instead of being skipped.
- Caravan ambushes no longer happen twice.
- Raid warnings now appear in your chosen language.
- The countdown and warning letter now show the same arrival time.
- Raids now attack together instead of splitting into small groups.
- Caravan ambushes no longer lose their leaders.
- Large raids no longer stand around after loading a save; they keep attacking.
